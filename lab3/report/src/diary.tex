\section{Work diary}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Stage 1: Study of the PATRICIA tree. Analysis of critical bits, back edges and the header
    node.
    \item Stage 2: Implementation of the dictionary. The operations \texttt{insert}, \texttt{find},
    \texttt{remove}, case-insensitive key handling and binary serialization were added.
    \item Stage 3: Preparation of the benchmark and the test generator
    (\texttt{lab3/benchmark/generator.py}). PATRICIA was compared with \texttt{std::map} on sets of
    $1\,000$ to $1\,000\,000$ keys.
    \item Stage 4: Quality research. \texttt{gprof}, Valgrind Memcheck and Valgrind Massif were
    used; data on execution time and memory were obtained.
    \item Stage 5: Fixing the defects. Errors in file loading, binary format checking, saving a key
    of length 256 and the lookup measurement in the benchmark were fixed.
    \item Stage 6: Rebuilding the report and a final check that the results are up to date.
\end{itemize}
