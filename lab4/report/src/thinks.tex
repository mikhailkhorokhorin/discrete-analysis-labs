\section{Conclusions}

In this laboratory work for the course \enquote{Discrete Analysis}
I implemented the Knuth--Morris--Pratt algorithm for finding a pattern in a text over the
alphabet of integers. I studied the construction of the prefix function and the fallback
mechanism that avoids repeated comparisons. I implemented correct handling of leading zeros
by parsing numbers into \texttt{uint32\_t}, reporting of invalid tokens instead of silent
truncation, and streaming search for occurrences that cross line boundaries without storing
the whole text in memory, using \texttt{KmpMatcher} with a ring buffer of the last $|P|$ positions.

Testing confirmed the theoretical estimates: on random data the advantage of KMP is not always
noticeable because partial matches are short, but on pathological inputs
(a pattern of 19 ones followed by a two in a text of ones) the algorithm is
about \textbf{4.7$\times$} faster than naive search for $|T| = 5\,000\,000$,
which illustrates the difference between $O(|P| + |T|)$ and $O(|P| \cdot |T|)$.
