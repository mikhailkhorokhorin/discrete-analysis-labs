\section{Conclusions}

In this laboratory work for the course \enquote{Discrete Analysis} I studied the structure and the
implementation of the PATRICIA tree. I learned the mechanism of critical bits and back edges and
implemented insertion, removal and search correctly with all boundary cases (removal of a leaf,
removal of an internal node, a single node in the tree). I implemented binary serialization with
format validation and atomic loading.

The benchmark against \texttt{std::map} showed that the overhead of PATRICIA does not pay off on
small dictionaries, while for $N \geq 500\,000$ keys PATRICIA becomes faster in most operations,
because the descent compares single critical bits instead of whole strings at every level.
Removal remains the most expensive operation because of the restoration of back edges.
